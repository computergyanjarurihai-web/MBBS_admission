How NEET counselling actually works

1
Two separate tracks
All-India Quota (AIQ) — 15% of government seats, plus deemed/central universities and AIIMS/JIPMER — via MCC. State quota — remaining 85% of government seats, plus most private seats — via each state's own authority.
2
Registration & documents
Register with NEET roll number, category certificate, domicile proof (state quota), ID proof. Pay the registration/security fee — refundable for AIQ if unallotted.
3
Choice filling
List colleges in your real order of preference. Edit until the deadline. This is where verified seat/fee/cutoff data actually changes decisions.
4
Allotment rounds
Round 1, Round 2, usually a Mop-Up and Stray Vacancy round. Each round: accept & report, or hold and try again if the round allows it.
5
Reporting & admission
Report in person within the window with original documents. Missing the deadline typically forfeits the seat.
Where to register: mcc.nic.in for AIQ/deemed/central universities/AIIMS/JIPMER. For state quota, search "[your state] NEET UG counselling" for that state's official portal.
