Disclaimer

This atlas is free and independent — no college pays for placement or a better position here, and no agent operates behind it. It is a data and comparison tool, not an application portal: real admissions happen only through NEET counselling. Figures carry verification badges — seats and NIRF ranks are verified against official sources; cutoffs and fees marked partial are modelled or pending verification, and gaps are shown as gaps. Category cutoffs vary every year and differ between the All-India and state quotas. Always confirm with the NMC (nmc.org.in), MCC (mcc.nic.in) and your state counselling authority before taking any decision.
